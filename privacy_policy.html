Privacy Policy for Goblin Miner: Gold Rush

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Summary: Goblin Miner: Gold Rush is designed for family and casual entertainment. We respect your privacy. This game DOES NOT collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from any user or child.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our game operates locally on your device. We do not require account registration or login. We do not collect:

  • Personal details (Name, Email, Phone number, Address)
  • Precise location or GPS data
  • Contacts, photos, or device microphone/camera data
  • Financial or payment credentials

2. Local Game Storage (Save Data)

Game progression, coin balances, level upgrades, and cosmetic unlocks are stored solely in the local sandbox storage of your device (e.g. user://save_game.json). This data is never transmitted to external servers and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our game is built to be fun and safe for players of all ages, including children:

  • We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU).
  • There is no open chat, messaging, or social networking feature within the game.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The current version of Goblin Miner: Gold Rush does not integrate third-party ad networks or user behavioral tracking tools. If non-personalized ads are introduced in future releases, they will strictly comply with Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

5. Security

We take all necessary reasonable measures to ensure that your local gaming experience is secure, malware-free, and respectful of your device permissions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL with an updated effective date.
